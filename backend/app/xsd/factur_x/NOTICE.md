# Factur-X schemas

The EN16931 XSD files under `en16931/` are Factur-X 1.09.2 schemas mirrored from the MIT-licensed `atgp/factur-x` project for local/offline validation inside Eye Supremo.

Source: `https://github.com/atgp/factur-x/tree/master/xsd/factur-x/en16931`

Eye Supremo does not use the PHP implementation. The schemas are loaded locally with network/entity resolution disabled.
